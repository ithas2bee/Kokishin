# Product Vision

## Vision

Kokishin is a learning management system that also serves as a practical teaching portfolio. It should let an instructor design and organize courses, guide learners through course material, assess their work, and demonstrate thoughtful teaching practice.

## Intended audience

- Instructors who need to organize and deliver courses
- Learners who need a clear place to find materials, activities, and feedback
- Academic hiring committees reviewing the project's teaching and course-design capabilities

## Product principles

1. Make course information and next steps easy to find.
2. Support a clear weekly or module-based course structure.
3. Make assignments, submissions, grading, and feedback understandable.
4. Keep the interface practical and familiar, informed by common LMS workflows without copying another product's branding.
5. Treat teaching practice and course design as central parts of the product.

## Product direction

The first release should establish a coherent end-to-end course experience. Additional features should be added only when they support the vision and can be implemented reliably.
